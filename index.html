Unisex Gym

Samrala Chowk & Gill Road, Ludhiana

Katyal Fitness Gym runs two unisex branches in Ludhiana — one on Tajpur Road behind Dashmesh Dairy at Samrala Chowk, the other on Gill Road. Men and women train on the same floor, with space for free weights, machines and cardio.

We keep membership simple, with options for teens starting out, individuals training on their own schedule, and families who would rather come in together. Whichever you pick, it works at both branches.
