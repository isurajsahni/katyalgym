About Us

Place To Transform.

Unisex Gym

Samrala Chowk, Ludhiana

Katyal Fitness Gym is a unisex gym on Tajpur Road, just behind Dashmesh Dairy at Samrala Chowk. Men and women train on the same floor, with space for free weights, machines and cardio all under one roof.

We keep membership simple, with options for teens starting out, individuals training on their own schedule, and families who would rather come in together. Whichever you pick, you get the full floor.
